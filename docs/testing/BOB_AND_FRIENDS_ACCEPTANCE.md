# BOB AND FRIENDS — HUMAN ACCEPTANCE

`Assets/bobandfriends.unity` is a human integration fixture. Do not replace this checklist with scene-YAML parsing.

## Basic fixture

1. Open `Assets/bobandfriends.unity`.
2. Confirm Bob, Alice, Charlie, and Dana are present.
3. Confirm Bob, Alice, and Charlie have their intended regular work assignments; Dana remains deliberately unassigned unless the fixture contract changes.
4. Confirm the four intended sleep positions/activities are visibly usable.
5. Confirm Cafeteria, Farm, Command work, and recreation activities are present.

## Play Mode smoke

1. Press Play.
2. Observe that each colonist advances independently.
3. Confirm assigned workers can physically reach and perform their work.
4. Confirm a sleepy colonist can physically reach and use its bed.
5. Confirm a hungry colonist can physically reach the Cafeteria when service is available.
6. Confirm only one colonist physically owns a one-seat reservation group at a time.
7. Confirm OffDuty activities can be used and visibly exit/release correctly.
8. Inspect structured logs when behavior is surprising; the log should explain decisions without becoming gameplay authority.

## When this checklist changes

Update this document when the intended fixture contract changes. Do not preserve obsolete fixture details in automated serialization tests.
